How every micro-campaign works
Blog + keyword harvest→ Blog draft→ Chris & Victoria approve→ Landing page, ads, guide & emails match the approved blog→ Google Ads ad group→ Landing page (Aspen agent builds)→ Guide download→ Aspen sequence→ Consultation booked
The approved blog is the master document. Once both owners approve it, every figure, step and consequence on the landing page, ads, guide and emails must match it. The Aspen prompts below embed the approved text automatically.
Tracked per campaign. The guide form sends campaign=<key> to /webhook/lead-magnet → Aspen. Calendly bookings carry utm_campaign and show up in the report automatically.
Guardrails. Spend in {{ plan.GEO }}. 90/10 estate planning: probate searches are the hook, living trusts are the offer. No "free consultation" in landing pages or emails.
